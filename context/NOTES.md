# Notes

The living friction ledger: rough edges, workarounds, open questions, and
observations worth keeping between sessions but not yet settled enough to be a
decision or a finding. Lower bar than the other registers — capture first,
promote later. When a note hardens into a ruling it moves to `DECISIONS.md`;
when it becomes a verified fact it moves to `FINDINGS.md`.
